FUELWISE · THE PITCH01 / 05
01 · The problem

One simple question.
Too many unknowns.

“What might my next road trip cost in fuel?”

Efficiency, distance, and gas prices all matter. Raw datasets are useful, but not particularly friendly to someone planning a drive.

FuelWise turns a dataset into a decision aid

  • Simple inputs, no uploads
  • Clear MPG averages and arithmetic
  • Instant updates as choices change
  • Documentation built into the Shiny app
THE USER'S QUESTION02 / 05
02 · The solution

Four controls. Live answers.

Selection4 · 6 · 8 cylinders
Vehicle typeAll · Auto · Manual
Trip length50–3,000 miles
Fuel price$1.50–$8.00

Server-side reactive calculation

mpg ← mean(selected cars' MPG) gallons ← miles ÷ mpg cost ← gallons × price

Every change recomputes the results and updates two charts from R Shiny's server logic.
